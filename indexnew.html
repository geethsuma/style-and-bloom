Good to know

Questions we are often asked

How long can I hire for?

Hire is for 24 or 48 hours. Your quote shows the exact collection and return times.

Do you deliver?

Yes, in Reading and nearby areas. A delivery fee applies and is shown in your quote.

Can I use the items outdoors?

Only if we have agreed it in writing. Electrical items such as neon signs and light-up numbers are for indoor use.

What if something gets damaged?

Normal wear and tear is fine. We take a refundable deposit and check everything when it comes back.

What if I need to cancel?

Our hire agreement sets out the charges, which depend on how far ahead of your event you cancel.
